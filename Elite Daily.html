Whenever Ariana Madix wants a quick escape to Fiji, all the Love Island host needs is a spritz of a Sol de Janeiro Intense Perfume Mist. “I’m always taken back there when I use these scents, because they have that sort of warm tropical vibe to them,” she says.

She carries one even while playing Connie Francis in Just In Time on Broadway, picking No. 59, 62, 91 or 74 to suit the occasion.

The 41-year-old also picked up a few beauty tricks from the islanders.
