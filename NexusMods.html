What if I told you that you could walk right past the southern border of Skyrim and step straight back into Cyrodiil?

Beyond Skyrim: Bruma isn't just a simple quest mod; it's practically a fully-fledged, unofficial expansion pack for a game that just refuses to stop growing. You see, it allows you to seamlessly cross the border into the familiar lands we explored in Oblivion and explore a reimagined, 4th-Era County Bruma that is actually larger and denser than the official Dragonborn DLC.

Oblivion veterans can look forward to Ayleid ruins, Frostcrag Spire, Cloud Ruler Temple and plenty of new quests.

Bruma was rebuilt from scratch with 70+ residents, 24,000+ voiced lines, 3+ hours of music and new Cyrodilic gear, playable with a new or existing character.

First released in 2017, the mod has now received a major 2.0 update.

Why a 2.0 release now?

The public build fell out of step with the Beyond Skyrim development team's internal work on the rest of Cyrodiil.

A merge begun last September clears that technical debt and readies the files for future Cyrodiil releases.

Highlights of the overhaul:

  • A Bigger, Better World: Larger exterior, new dungeons, reworked older areas.
  • Fresh Gear & Foes: More Cyrodilic weapons, clothing and armour, plus new enemies, spells and bosses.
  • Restored Content & Massive Polish: Restored cut quests, hundreds of fixes, Survival Mode, custom weather, treasure maps and rebalancing.
  • PlayStation Support & Backend Magic: PlayStation support, enabled by a new release-building tool.

Note that 2.0 is not compatible with old Bruma saves: use a pre-Bruma save or start fresh.

The team is now building the rest of Cyrodiil, up to the Imperial City, and is looking for Creation Kit experts, 3D modellers, concept artists and landscape designers; see the official website or the application form.

Beginners can learn the ropes at The Arcane University Discord.

Cyrodiil awaits.
