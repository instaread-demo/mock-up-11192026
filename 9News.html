Five teens charged with murdering young woman in Queensland drive-by shooting

Patrick Brischetto

Five teenage boys have been charged with murder over the deadly shooting of a young woman south of Brisbane last month.

The charges come after Queensland Police conducted several raids on homes in the Logan area on Thursday as officers continue to investigate the shooting death of 19-year-old Vana-Reese Schuster-Togiatau outside a Loganholme pub on October 24.

Five boys, all aged 16, were arrested.

Three were charged on Thursday afternoon and the other two that night, each with murder, arson and three counts of attempted murder.

Detective Inspector Ian Galpin called it the first stage of the investigation, saying police are still examining whether anyone else was involved.

Police allege two of the accused were in the vehicle during the attack, while Galpin said a third had a different role.

Schuster-Togiatau was one of four people shot outside Fitzy’s Loganholme during a boxing event on October 24. She died from her injuries four days later, and police believe all four were bystanders.

Galpin said he hoped the arrests would bring some comfort to her family, the other victims and the community.

About 50 officers, including homicide detectives, searched five homes and several vehicles on Thursday and seized evidence.

The weapon has not been found and no motive has been established. Galpin said it was too early to know whether gangs were involved, adding that police were keeping “an open mind”.

All five are due to appear in Brisbane Childrens Court on Friday.
