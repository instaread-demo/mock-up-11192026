New Milestone Rewards

Hyatt has added a number of new milestone rewards, including milestone rewards starting at 10 nights or 20,000 base points.

  • Inclusive Collection Spa Credit: $30 for spa treatments at Inclusive Collection resorts, yours or as a gift.
  • Tier-Qualifying Night Credits: Selectable in blocks of 1, 2 or 5 to reach status sooner.
  • Thompson Hotels Credit: $200 off eligible paid Thompson stays.
  • Premium Suite Credit: $500 toward a premium suite on paid stays of 2+ nights.
  • Free Parking Award: Parking covered for up to seven nights on qualifying stays.

Combinable Awards

Suite Upgrade, Guest of Honor and Free Parking awards can be stacked on one stay.

Award Expiry

All awards now last 365 days, versus one year (Chase) or six months (Hyatt) before.

New Delta Rewards

Previously teased; details are now out.

Globalists Lose Free Parking On Award Stays

Globalists lose free award-stay parking (from February 2028 for this year's qualifiers). The author doubts Hyatt's usage claim, citing Greg from FM's 10+ uses a year.

Our Verdict

William Charles doubts the 70% claim and wants the perk kept, but likes stackable awards and consistent expiry.
