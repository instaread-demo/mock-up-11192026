Turks and Caicos Poised for a Record-Breaking Winter

by Noreen Kompanik Last updated: 11:54 AM ET, Thu November 19, 2026

Turks and Caicos is eyeing its strongest winter yet with record growth, expanded flight options and luxury hotel openings.

According to the destination tourism authority, Experience Turks and Caicos, the island is cementing its status as one of the Caribbean’s most sought-after escapes for winter 2026 and 2027 travel.

Record Demand & Winter Momentum

  • Nine Straight Months of Growth: 514,670 stayover arrivals from January to September 2026, up 5%.
  • Strong Winter Projections: December +2.3%, January +4%, February +12.8%, March +7.7%.
  • Top Festive Ranking: Virtuoso ranks the islands #4 globally for the holidays.

New & Expanded Flights

  • First-Ever Nonstop Flight from Denver: United’s DEN–PLS route begins December 19, 2026.
  • First-Ever Nonstop Flight from Newark to South Caicos: BermudAir flies it from December 20, 2026, to April 25, 2027.
  • Expanded Access Across Six U.S. Gateways: BermudAir links Providenciales with Newark, Boston, BWI, Raleigh-Durham, Fort Lauderdale and St. Pete-Clearwater, October 2026 to May 2027.

Luxury Hotel Portfolio Expansion

Andaz Turks & Caicos at Grace Bay opens in spring 2027, with Kimpton Turks & Caicos and St. Regis, Turks & Caicos, The Residences to follow later that year.
