The 24th fishing tourney of Pocket Camp Complete (104th between both versions) is here: Bottled!

From November 10th until November 19th, Chip Jr. is visiting Saltwater Shores. He's looking for creatures, and you're just the person to catch them for him. Three new creatures are temporarily available during this event:

  • Bigfin Reef Squid
  • Red King Crab
  • Yellowfin Tuna

Event creatures have sparkling shadows. Chip Jr. measures each catch, and combined-length goals unlock prizes ranging from anniversary candles and furniture to essences and Leaf Tokens.

A rented golden rod guarantees double catches, and tourney nets land six fish from the pier.

Chip Jr. Challenges add more Leaf Tokens, Sparkle Stones and event items.

Four trophies mark the total-length milestones:

  • Wood: 20 cm to 2,499.9 cm
  • Bronze: 2,500.0 cm to 4,399.9 cm
  • Silver: 4,400.0 cm to 9,699.9 cm
  • Gold: 9,700.0 cm or above

Time to start fishing.
