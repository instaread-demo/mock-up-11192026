This 1994 Ford Thunderbird runs the 4.6L V8 and, according to the seller, has spent its entire life in Washington and Oregon, so there's not a speck of rust on it. It carries an unusual ownership chain: the seller says Jim Waldo of Waldo Drag Racing bought it new in 1994 for his wife, after which it passed to the Waldos' grandkids and then to the seller's own kids. The plastic odometer broke at 71,000 miles, but the seller estimates roughly 100,000 total miles. It is located in Portland, Oregon.
